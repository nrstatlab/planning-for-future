# Content check, November 2026

Every page is read in full, section by section, for four things: the definition is correct,
the topic is in the right place, each example fits the topic it stands under, and the working
is right (numbers recomputed, conclusions follow). Clear mistakes are corrected at once and
listed here; matters of judgement are listed under "For the owner" and left unchanged.

Recompute scripts are in `tools/audit/2026-11/`. R and SPSS are not installed on the machine
used for the check, so R output is confirmed by reproducing the same numbers in Python.

Order: Statistics courses only sampled in September, then Data Science, then a second reading
of the courses read in September, then the exam pages, then the guides and the calculator.
