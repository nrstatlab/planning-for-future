    Exam Subjects

    The allied subjects statistics papers ask for, written to their syllabus lines

    On this page
    1. The subjects
    2. Why these are not in the statistics section
    3. What these pages will not tell you

    Statistics recruitment papers do not only ask about statistics. The APPSC Assistant Director and Assistant Statistical Officer papers put economics, financial accounting and computing in the same paper as central tendency and index numbers; so does the Indian Statistical Service. This section holds those allied subjects — written to the syllabus lines that ask for them, and linked to from every map that needs them.

    The subjects

    6 UNITS

    Financial Accounting

    Concepts and conventions, journal and ledger, subsidiary books, bank reconciliation, trial balance and final accounts, depreciation and incomplete records. Units 2 to 5 follow one firm through one month, so the cash balance computed in the ledger is the cash balance on the balance sheet three units later.

     6 UNITS

    Economics

    Price determination and market structures, national income and the national accounts, money and banking, public finance, international economics, and the Indian economy. The national income unit computes the same GDP three ways and requires them to agree.

    Why these are not in the statistics section

    A deliberate separation

    The statistics section is statistics. Putting economics and accounting in it would make that untrue, and someone browsing for statistics subjects would find two that are not.

    Putting them under one exam’s folder instead — inside the APPSC map — would have been worse, because the Indian Statistical Service asks for national income too, and a second exam linking into the first exam’s folder invites a second copy of the same notes. The site’s rule is that notes are shared, not copied: correct a page once and every map pointing at it is corrected. A neutral section is what makes that possible.

    What these pages will not tell you

    No current figures, on purpose

    You will not find this year’s growth rate, the current repo rate, the latest poverty headcount or any other moving number on these pages. Every rupee amount is either derived in front of you or labelled as an illustration built for that example.

    That is the same rule the examinations section applies to marks and exam patterns, and for the same reason: a stale figure in front of someone planning months of study is worse than no figure at all. What these pages teach is the structure — how a deficit is defined, why three estimates of national income must reconcile, how a bank reconciliation closes — which does not change between notifications. For current data, go to the source that publishes it.
