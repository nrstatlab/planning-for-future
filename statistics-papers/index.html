No exam pattern is stated here, and that is deliberate. Marks, duration, negative marking, the number of papers and eligibility all change between notifications, and a stale figure in front of someone planning months of study is worse than no figure at all. Nothing of that kind appears on this site unless it was taken from the official document for that exam, and the page says which document and when.

Always read the current official notification for the exam you are sitting. Use this section for the statistics, not for the rules.

How this section works

  1. The syllabus map is the thing. Each exam page takes that exam's official topic list and points each line at the page here that teaches it — so you read in the exam's order, not the textbook's.
  2. The gaps are named. Every map carries a list of what this site does not cover for that exam. A map that quietly skips its holes is worse than no map, because you only find out in the hall.
  3. The notes are shared, not copied. A map links into the study sections below. Correct a proof once and every exam that points at it is corrected too.
  4. Papers get solved, not listed. Where a previous paper is available it is worked through, the way the 2026 paper already is.
