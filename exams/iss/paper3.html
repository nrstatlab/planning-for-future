    Statistics-III — the syllabus, line by line

    Descriptive · 200 marks · 3 hrs · 30 syllabus lines, 83% covered here

    Paper III of the Indian Statistical Service written examination is Statistics-Iii, and this page takes its syllabus one line at a time. Each line is reproduced as it is prescribed, then pointed at the page on this site that teaches it, with the depth stated rather than implied.
