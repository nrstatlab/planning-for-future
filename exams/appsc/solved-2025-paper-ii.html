    Q1.

    A statement has been given below followed by two conclusions. Assuming given statement to be true which of the following options will be correct for given conclusions.

    Statement: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) increases the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) to reduce excess liquidity in the economy.

    Conclusions:

    I. An increase in CRR leads to a decrease in the lending capacity of banks.

    II. An increase in CRR results in higher money supply in the economy.

    1. Only Conclusion 1 follows
    2. Only Conclusion 2 follows
    3. Both 1 and 2 follow
    4. Neither 1 nor 2 follows
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Only Conclusion 1 follows

    A higher CRR locks more of every deposit away with the RBI, so banks have less to lend (I follows). Less lending means less credit creation and so a smaller money supply, the opposite of II.

    Q15.

    Demand of good or service is said to be elastic if its elasticity satisfies which of the following conditions?

    1. Ed<1
    2. Ed=1
    3. Ed>1
    4. Ed=0
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Ed>1

    Demand is elastic when $|E_d| > 1$ — quantity responds more than proportionately to price. $E_d = 1$ is unit elastic, $E_d < 1$ inelastic, $E_d = 0$ perfectly inelastic.
