    Economics 43 questions · Q1–Q59

    Q1.

    “Production is the outcome of the combined activity of the four factors of production”.

    With reference to the above statement, which of the following is/are NOT considered as factors of production?

    (i) Land

    (ii) Labour

    (iii) Capital

    (iv) Organisation

    (v) Service

    1. (i) only
    2. (ii) only
    3. (i), (ii) and (iii) only
    4. (v) only
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) (v) only

    The four factors of production are land, labour, capital and organisation (enterprise); each earns rent, wages, interest and profit. A service is an output, not a factor.

    Study this: National Income and the National Accounts — Economics (Unit 2)

    Q2.

    Two goods X and Y are said to be ______ if the price of good X is inversely related to the demand of good Y.

    1. substitutes
    2. complementary
    3. Giffen goods
    4. necessities
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) complementary

    Complements are used together (car and petrol): a rise in the price of X lowers the demand for Y. For substitutes the relation runs the other way — a dearer X raises the demand for Y.

    Study this: Price Determination, Market Structures and Factor Incomes — Economics (Unit 1)

    Q3.

    Which of the following statements are true/false?

    (i) Inferior goods have positive income effect.

    2. Statement (i) is true and statement (ii) is false
    3. Both statements (i) and (ii) are false
    4. Statement (i) is false and statement (ii) is true
