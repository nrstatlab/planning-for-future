    Model Questions — UGC NET Paper I

    UGC NET Paper I (General Paper) · 150 questions · fifty on each unit, every answer worked out

    About these questions

    Model questions written at the standard of the paper, unit by unit: problems that take several steps, “which of the statements are correct”, match List I with List II, and assertion and reason, as well as direct questions. Each one has four options and one right answer, and Show Answer gives the full working, often with the reason the tempting wrong option is wrong, and a link to the notes.

    They are written for practice and are not questions from a past paper. For shorter recall questions use the short model MCQs; for the real thing, work through Paper I of June 2026.

    Units

    • Unit 1 — Teaching Aptitude50 questions · Teaching and its levels, learners and their differences, the factors that affect teaching, methods off-line and on-line, teaching support systems, and evaluation, including the Choice Based Credit System and computer-based testing.
    • Unit 2 — Research Aptitude50 questions · The meaning and types of research, positivism and post-positivism, experimental, descriptive and historical methods, qualitative and quantitative approaches, the steps of research, thesis writing and referencing, ICT in research, and research ethics.
    • Unit 3 — Comprehension50 questions · Ten passages, each followed by five questions of the kinds the paper sets: the main idea, a stated detail, an inference, the meaning of a word in its context, the author's attitude and the best title.

    The remaining units are added as they are written and checked.
