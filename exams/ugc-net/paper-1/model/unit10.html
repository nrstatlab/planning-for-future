    Model Questions — Unit 10: Higher Education System

    UGC NET Paper I (General Paper) · 50 questions · Click "Show Answer" for the worked solution

    About this set

    Institutions of higher learning in ancient India, higher education since Independence, oriental, conventional and non-conventional programmes, professional, technical and skill-based education, value and environmental education, and policies, governance and administration.

    These are model questions written for practice at the standard of the paper; they are not questions from a past paper. Try each one before opening the answer. Every answer gives the working and a link to the section of the Unit 10 notes that teaches it.

    The questions

    Q1. Match the ancient centre of learning in List I with the ruler or dynasty under whom it was founded in List II.
    List IList II
    (a) Nalanda(i) Gopala (Pala)
    (b) Vikramashila(ii) The Maitraka kings
    (c) Odantapuri(iii) Kumaragupta I (Gupta)
    (d) Valabhi(iv) Dharmapala (Pala)
    1. (a)-(iii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(ii)
    2. (a)-(iii), (b)-(iv), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii)
    3. (a)-(iv), (b)-(iii), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii)
    4. (a)-(iii), (b)-(iv), (c)-(ii), (d)-(i)

    Answer: (B) (a)-(iii), (b)-(iv), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii).

    Nalanda was founded in the 5th century under Kumaragupta I; Vikramashila by Dharmapala and Odantapuri by Gopala, both Pala kings, in the 8th century; Valabhi flourished under the Maitrakas of Gujarat.

    Q2. Which of the following ancient centres of learning lies outside the present-day territory of India?

    2. Vikramashila
    3. Valabhi
    4. Takshashila

    Answer: (D) Takshashila.

    Takshashila (Taxila) is near Rawalpindi in present-day Pakistan. Nalanda, Vikramashila and Odantapuri are in Bihar and Valabhi in Gujarat.

    Q3. Consider the statements about Nalanda:(I) It was a great residential Buddhist university.(II) The Chinese pilgrim Xuanzang studied there.(III) Its ruins are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.(IV) It was founded by the Pala king Dharmapala.Which of the statements are correct?

    Nalanda was founded under the Gupta king Kumaragupta I; it was Vikramashila that Dharmapala founded. So (IV) is false. The ruins of Nalanda were inscribed as a World Heritage Site in 2016.

    Q6. Which of the following was NOT a feature of the gurukula system?

    Q45. An autonomous college is one that:

    2. is not affiliated to any university

    4. receives no grants from the government
