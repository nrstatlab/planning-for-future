    About NRSTATLAB

    Free study material for Statistics, Data Science and the competitive examinations that test them — unit notes, worked examples, practice with solutions, and every lab program as code you can run.

    Who writes it

    The material is written and maintained by NRSTATLAB. It is free to read, needs no sign-in, and works on a phone.

    How the material is written

    Four rules, applied to every page:

    • Every step is shown. No “clearly” or “it follows that” standing in for a step. Proofs name the tool each line uses; problems show the setup, the substitution and the arithmetic.
    • Examples, not just results. Worked examples throughout, and at least two per concept in the exam material — the answer always ends with what it actually means.
    • The code runs. Lab programs are real source in C, Python, SQL and R, checked by compiling and running them rather than by reading them.
    • Gaps are stated. Where a syllabus omits something the exam still asks, it is covered and flagged. Where something was not verified, that is said plainly instead of hidden.

    Examination details

    The rule on this site is that a page states the marks, duration, pattern or eligibility of an examination only when the figure comes from an official document, and names that document. The syllabus maps reproduce each syllabus line as it is prescribed, point it at the page that teaches it, and grade how deeply that page covers it — the lines this site does not yet teach are listed, not hidden.

    Notifications change. Always confirm the details of an examination against its current official notice before you rely on them. If you find a page that breaks this rule, please report it.

    Report an error

    Corrections are welcome and are the fastest way this material gets better. Open an issue on the repository and include:

    • the address of the page;
    • the passage, formula or answer that is wrong;
    • what you believe it should say, and why — a reference helps.

    Opening an issue needs a free GitHub account. The same address is the place to ask for permission beyond the licence below.
