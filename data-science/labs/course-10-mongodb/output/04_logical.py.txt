Experiment 4 -- Logical operators
  implicit AND == explicit $and; $and needed for same-field clauses
  $or (Stats OR maths>85) -> ['Asha', 'Bhanu', 'Meena']
  $nor [dept=DS, age=20] -> ['Bhanu'], identical to (NOT A) AND (NOT B)
  $not inverts an OPERATOR EXPRESSION; a plain value is an error
  nested $and/$or -> ['Asha', 'Kiran', 'Meena']
