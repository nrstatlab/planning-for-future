Experiment 11 -- Modelling relationships
  1-to-1: EMBED. One read; the sub-document is still queryable by dot
  1-to-few: embed as an ARRAY -- bounded, so no 16 MB risk
  1-to-many: reference from the CHILD. 1000 more enrolments left the
       course document at 88 chars -- an embedded array would
       have grown it, and eventually breached 16 MB
  M-to-M: student 21 takes ['DSC301', 'STA302']; DSC301 has students [21, 22]
  the GRADE lives on the enrolment, not on the student or the course
       -- exactly the reasoning that produces a junction TABLE in
       Course 5, and it survives the translation unchanged
