(a) (b) (c) Bold, italics and underline

Bold with <strong> and bold with <b>.
Italic with <em> and italic with <i>.
Underlined with <u> and underlined with CSS.

<strong> and <em> are semantic — they mean "important" and "emphasised", and a screen reader changes its tone for them. <b> and <i> are purely presentational. Prefer the semantic ones.

(g) Paragraphs, (h) line breaks, (i) horizontal rule

This is one paragraph. A paragraph is a block element, so the browser puts space above and below it automatically.

Line one
Line two, after a <br>
Line three

Use <br> only where the line break is part of the content — an address, a poem. Never <br><br> for spacing; that is what CSS margins are for.
