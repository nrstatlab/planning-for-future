  Email  --  Allow: Custom,  =ISNUMBER(SEARCH("@",E2))
    value                   syllabus rule   stricter rule
    asha@nri.ac.in          accepted        accepted
    ASHA@NRI.AC.IN          accepted        accepted
    @                       accepted        REJECTED
    not an email @ all      accepted        REJECTED
    asha.nri.ac.in          REJECTED        REJECTED

  Both rules accept a real address and reject one with no @.
  The difference is '@' on its own, and a sentence with an @ in
  it: the syllabus rule takes both. Say so in the viva -- knowing
  the limits of your own validation is the point of the exercise.

  Every rule needs all three tabs filled in:
    Settings        the rule itself
    Input Message   the hint shown when the cell is selected
    Error Alert     Stop / Warning / Information, and your text

  Stop refuses the value. Warning and Information both ALLOW it
  after a confirmation -- so a form that must not accept bad data
  has to use Stop.
