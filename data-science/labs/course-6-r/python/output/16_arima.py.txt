  💡 THE LESSON: seasonality was NOT visible in the raw ACF, because
  the trend swamped it. Only after differencing does the oscillation
  appear -- negative around lag 6, peaking again at lag 12. This is
  why the order of operations matters: difference FIRST, then read
  the ACF and PACF. Reading them on a trending series tells you
  almost nothing except 'there is a trend'.
