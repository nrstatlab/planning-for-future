About This Study Material

This study material covers the entire UGC NET Statistics syllabus (Subject Code 107) prescribed by the University Grants Commission. Each unit contains complete theoretical content, formulas typeset with MathJax, definitions, theorems, and at least two worked examples per concept to build deep understanding.

Use the navigation above to jump to any unit. Mathematical expressions are rendered using MathJax — they may take a moment to load on first view.

How to use this guide:
  • Read each unit sequentially — earlier units (probability, real analysis) underpin later units.
  • Re-derive every formula on paper after reading; statistics is best learned by doing.
  • Solve both examples in each section before moving on.
  • Revise the highlighted theorems just before the exam.

All 10 Units

UNIT I

Probability and Distributions

Probability axioms, Bayes' theorem, random variables, MGF, standard distributions, convergence, CLT, LLN.
UNIT II

Real Analysis & Matrix Algebra

Sequences, series, continuity, differentiation, integration, vector spaces, eigenvalues, quadratic forms.
UNIT III

Sampling Methods & Design of Experiments

SRS, stratified, cluster, systematic, ratio & regression estimation, ANOVA, CRD, RBD, LSD, BIBD.
UNIT IV

Estimation Theory

Unbiasedness, MLE, MoM, UMVUE, Cramér–Rao, sufficiency, Rao–Blackwell, Lehmann–Scheffé, CIs.
UNIT V

Testing of Hypotheses

Neyman–Pearson, UMP tests, LRT, SPRT, chi-square, sign, Wilcoxon, Mann–Whitney, Kruskal–Wallis.
UNIT VI

Linear Estimation, Regression & Econometrics

Gauss–Markov, OLS, GLS, dummy variables, multicollinearity, heteroscedasticity, autocorrelation, 2SLS.
UNIT VII

Time Series

ACF, PACF, stationarity, AR, MA, ARMA, ARIMA, Yule–Walker, forecasting, spectral density.
UNIT VIII

Multivariate Analysis

Multivariate normal, Wishart, Hotelling's $T^2$, discriminant, principal components, canonical correlation.
UNIT IX

Stochastic Processes

Markov chains, Chapman–Kolmogorov, Gambler's ruin, Poisson process, birth–death, M/M/1 queues.
UNIT X

Indian Statistical System & Research Methodology

MoSPI, NSO, NSC, Indian statisticians, R programming, LaTeX basics.

Exam Strategy & Tips

  • Pattern: NET Paper II has 100 MCQs; conceptual clarity beats rote memorization.
  • High-yield units: Probability (I), Estimation (IV), Testing (V), Regression (VI) jointly contribute the largest share of questions.
  • Formulas: Maintain a single revision sheet for distributions, MGFs, sampling-design variances, and test statistics.
  • R & LaTeX: Practice basic R syntax; even one direct R question is "free marks" if you have written code.
  • Indian statisticians: Memorise primary contributions of Mahalanobis, Bose, Roy, C R Rao, Sukhatme — Unit X questions are factual.
