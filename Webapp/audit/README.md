# Concept audit data (October 2026)

Generated from the site by the audit's sheet builder, and imported once into the Google Sheet
"StatsTricks360 – Concept audit", where the audit itself is recorded (status, findings, fixes).

- `topics.csv`: one row per main section (h2) of every statistics and examination study page, with
  its subsections.
- `pages.csv`: every study page and its address.
- `duplicates.csv`: topics taught in more than one course, and the course pairs to be judged for
  merging.

These files are a snapshot for the import; the sheet is the working record.
