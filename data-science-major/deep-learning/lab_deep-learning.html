IN DEPTH

On Keras and TensorFlow

The syllabus says Keras/TensorFlow. TensorFlow is not installed here, but Keras 3 is backend-agnostic — KERAS_BACKEND=torch runs the identical API on PyTorch. The lab prints Keras 3.15.1, backend 'torch', and every Sequential, .compile() and .fit() is the real thing.

The real MNIST, a stratified 4,000-image subset (400 per class), 1,000 test.

WATCH OUT

⚠️ Two results that contradict the usual story

VGG16 lost to a small CNN trained from scratch, and fine-tuning made MobileNetV2 worse. Both are reported as measured and explained in Unit 5 §5.3.

Running it yourself

pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
KERAS_BACKEND=torch python3 tools/run_deeplearning_labs.py

The datasets download once and cache under KERAS_HOME. On CPU the full suite takes roughly half an hour; individual scripts run in one to five minutes each.
