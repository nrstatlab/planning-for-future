    How this material is written

    Every step is shown

    No "clearly" or "it follows that" standing in for a step. Proofs name the tool each line uses; problems show the setup, the substitution and the arithmetic.

    Examples, not just results

    Worked examples throughout, and at least two per concept in the exam material — the answer always ends with what it actually means.

    The code runs

    Lab programs are real source in C, Python, SQL and R, checked by compiling and running them rather than by reading them.

    Gaps are stated

    Where a syllabus omits something the exam still asks, it is covered and flagged. Where something was not verified, that is said plainly instead of hidden.
