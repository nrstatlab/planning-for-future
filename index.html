How to use this

Pick the section that matches what you are studying and open it — each one has its own contents page listing every unit. Nothing here needs installing or signing in; it is all plain pages that work offline once loaded, and on a phone as well as a laptop.

The lab programs in the Data Science section are real source files. Open one to read it, or download the folder and run it.
